This month

The reading room is hosting an exhibition of local photographs from the county archive, covering the rebuilding of the harbour between 1946 and 1958. Entry is free and no booking is required.

Borrowing

Membership is free for anyone living or working in the district. Bring proof of address to the front desk and a card will be issued the same day. Members may borrow up to twelve items at a time and renew online twice before an item is due back.

Study rooms

Four quiet study rooms are available on the first floor and may be reserved up to two weeks ahead. The larger room seats eight and is used by reading groups on Tuesday evenings.

Northfield Public Library, 14 Harbour Road. Telephone 01555 0142.
